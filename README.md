## 1. Proposed Laboratory Philosophy

The laboratory is redesigned from a conventional algorithm-programming laboratory into an **AI implementation, evaluation and application laboratory**. Students should progress through the cycle:

$$\text{Understand} \longrightarrow \text{Formulate} \longrightarrow \text{Implement} \longrightarrow \text{Visualize} \longrightarrow \text{Use AI} \longrightarrow \text{Verify} \longrightarrow \text{Compare} \longrightarrow \text{Apply}$$

- **Implement Underlying Algorithms**: Implement the underlying AI algorithm rather than treating an AI tool as a black box.
- **Visualization & Tracing**: Use visualization and tracing tools to understand algorithm behaviour.
- **Generative AI Collaboration**: Use generative AI assistants for explanation, debugging, test-case generation and alternative approaches.
- **Human Supervision & Verification**: Verify AI-generated explanations, code and references before accepting them.
- **Empirical Evaluation**: Measure performance using appropriate metrics such as time, memory, nodes expanded, accuracy, reward or retrieval quality.
- **Real-World Application**: Apply classical AI concepts to realistic engineering and campus problems.
- **Modern Workflows**: Introduce modern AI workflows such as transformers, computer vision, RAG and responsible AI.

---

## 2. Course Objectives

- **Practical Competence**: Develop practical competence in implementing classical and modern artificial intelligence techniques.
- **Problem Formulation**: Enable students to formulate real-world problems as intelligent-agent, search, reasoning, learning, NLP and vision problems.
- **Visualization & Evaluation**: Develop the ability to visualize, trace, compare and evaluate AI algorithms.
- **Responsible AI Usage**: Introduce responsible use of generative AI tools as learning, coding, debugging and verification assistants.
- **Modern AI Exposure**: Expose students to modern AI applications including transformers, YOLO, reinforcement learning, RAG and multimodal systems.

## 5. AI-Assisted Laboratory Rule

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **AI-Assisted Laboratory Policy:**
> AI tools may be used as learning, explanation, debugging, visualization, test-case and verification assistants.
> 
> **Students shall NOT submit AI-generated code blindly.**
> Students must understand the solution, execute it, test it against expected outputs, correct errors independently and document how AI was used.
